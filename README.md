# EarlyWarn

### Turning Early Signals into Timely Action

EarlyWarn is an explainable early-warning and intervention platform designed to identify college students showing early signs of dropout risk.

The platform brings together relevant academic and engagement indicators to identify emerging risk, explain the factors contributing to the risk, and help faculty and counsellors take timely, data-informed action.

---

## Problem Statement

### Omni_EdTech_2 — Early Identification of At-Risk Dropout Students

Students who eventually drop out often show warning signs that may go unnoticed until it is too late to intervene.

EarlyWarn addresses this problem by identifying emerging risk patterns earlier and connecting risk insights with timely educator intervention.

---

## Proposed Solution

EarlyWarn combines **trend analysis, rule-based indicators and ML-based risk assessment** to identify students showing emerging signs of dropout risk.

The system generates:

- Low / Medium / High risk levels
- Key factors contributing to the identified risk
- Student risk trends over time
- Actionable insights for faculty and counsellors
- Intervention tracking and follow-up

The goal is not only to identify risk, but also to help educators understand the warning signs and take appropriate action.

---

## Key Features

### 🎯 Early Risk Identification
Identifies students showing emerging patterns associated with dropout risk.

### 📊 Multi-Indicator Risk Assessment
Considers relevant academic and engagement indicators for risk assessment.

### 🧠 Rule-Based + ML Risk Assessment
Combines predefined warning indicators with machine-learning-based risk assessment.

### 🔍 Explainable Risk Factors
Shows the key factors contributing to an individual student's risk assessment.

### 📋 Faculty & Counsellor Dashboard
Provides an actionable dashboard to help educators prioritize students requiring attention.

### 📈 Risk Trajectory Monitoring
Tracks whether student risk is improving, remaining stable, or worsening.

### 🤝 Intervention Tracking
Records interventions and supports follow-up after student support is provided.
